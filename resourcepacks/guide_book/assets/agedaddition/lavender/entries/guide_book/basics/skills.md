Press <keybind;key.levelz.openskillscreen> to open the skill screen.


You will see 12 different skill which you can level up.


Click on the skill icon to open up the sub skill screen.


Press "{gold}E{}" to return to the main skill screen.

;;;;;

On the top right of the skill screen are various books.

One opens the Attributes, the other the crafting recipes which are locked behind certain skills.


Next to your character you see you current xp progress for the next level.
