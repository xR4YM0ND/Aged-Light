There are 2 temperature indicators in the HUD.


First of the outside temperature gauge.

This tells you the temperature of the environment you are currently in.


;;;;;

Then the is the mannequin in the middle of your experience bar.


This tells you your body temperature wich raises or falls according to the outside temperature, soaked, wet, dimension, effects and items held.

;;;;;

**Cool down**

---

- Jump in water (wet/soaked)
- wear no armor or non affecting (cold) armor
- use ice pack or hold cold items
- avoid direct sunlight
- be next to cold blocks
- be in cold dimensions

;;;;;

**Heat up**

---

- wear insolating armor or warm armor
- use heating stones or hold hot items
- be in direct sunlight
- be next to hot blocks
- be in hot dimensions
