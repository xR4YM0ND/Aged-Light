Instead of leather, animals drop hide which can be prepared and turned into leather.

To do that get yourself a rack and place it on a wall.

<recipe;fleshz:oak_wood_rack>

;;;;;

You can also use rotten flesh to duplicate leather.

<recipe;fleshz:rotten_leather>

Drying it on a rack takes 15 minutes and it turns into Hide.

<item;fleshz:hide>

;;;;;

Prepared your hide.

<recipe;fleshz:prepared_hide>

Drying it on a rack takes 10 minutes and it turns into Leather.

<item;minecraft:leather>

;;;;;
