Farmland is the foundation of your crops, but keeping it productive takes water and the right tools.

;;;;;

Choose a setup that suits your farm:

- water by hand
- automate irrigation with sprinklers
- use fertilized farmland

;;;;;

**Watering Cans**

---

Watering cans let you irrigate crops by hand.

They’re useful for small plots, newly planted fields, and areas that your sprinklers don’t reach.

Keep a can filled and water your crops as needed.
